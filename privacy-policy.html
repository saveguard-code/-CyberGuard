Data Protection & Telemetry Protocols

At CyberGuard & Media, accessible from our primary domain, your privacy and data protocol security are paramount. This document outlines the types of information collected, logged, and processed by our system.

1. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Ads

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our platform. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our website visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

2. Advertising Partners Privacy Policies

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on CyberGuard. These are automatically sent to users' browsers, receiving your IP address in the process. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and/or to personalize advertising content.

3. Information We Collect

CyberGuard operates primarily on local client-side processing (such as client-side URL verification and randomized password generation). We do not collect personal identification information unless explicitly provided by the user via active communication channels.

4. Log Files

CyberGuard follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting services do this as part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.

5. Third-Party Links & Affiliates

Our website contains links to external platforms, including Redbubble store collections and utility services. We hold no responsibility for the content, privacy practices, or data collection policies of external third-party entities.

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its Terms and Conditions.
